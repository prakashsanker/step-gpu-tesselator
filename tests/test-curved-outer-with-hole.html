Testing Curved Outer Boundary with Hole

This tests if earcut handles planar faces where the outer boundary is curved (not LINE edges).

Test 1: Circular Annulus (Circle with Circular Hole)

Outer: circle (64 vertices), Hole: smaller circle (32 vertices)

Test 2: Rounded Hexagon with Circular Hole

Outer: 6 arcs forming rounded hexagon (~192 vertices), Hole: circle (64 vertices)

Test 3: Simple Hexagon with Circular Hole (control)

Outer: 6 LINE edges (6 vertices), Hole: circle (64 vertices)
